01 - About

I'm a first-year AI & Business student at Scaler School of Technology in Bangalore. Most of my time outside class goes into a simple question: how do I stop being average? Not in a dramatic way — I just don't want to coast through college doing the expected minimum and call it a degree.

That question is what pushed me toward building something real instead of just studying for it. So I went through real life problems and ended up building

That became Kevo↗ — a direct farm-to-home marketplace where farmers keep the margin a middleman would normally take. It's rough, mostly vibe-coded, and I'm not fully sure everything on it works the way it should — but it's the first thing I've built and put in front of real people instead of just planning it on paper.

Also I have built PAISO↗ to solve a simple problem I had myself — keeping track of everyday expenses without relying on scattered notes or remembering where my money went.

On the technical side, I'm still early — learning Java, HTML and CSS in class right now — and treating that as one of my two main priorities alongside academics. I'd rather learn by building imperfect things in public than wait until I feel "ready enough" to start.
