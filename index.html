01 - About

I'm a first-year AI & Business student at Scaler School of Technology in Bangalore. Most of my time outside class goes into a simple question: how do I stop being average? Not in a dramatic way — I just don't want to coast through college doing the expected minimum and call it a degree.

That question is what pushed me toward building something real instead of just studying for it. I went through two startup ideas that didn't work — one my tutor called too complicated to build, the other turned out to already exist in the market. Instead of giving up on the idea of building something, I looked at what I actually cared about: food safety and farming, and whether the people growing our food were getting a fair deal.

That became Kevo — a direct farm-to-home marketplace where farmers keep the margin a middleman would normally take. It started as a pitch-competition submission and turned into an actual live pilot in Nashik. It's rough, mostly vibe-coded, and I'm not fully sure everything on it works the way it should — but it's the first thing I've built and put in front of real people instead of just planning it on paper.

On the technical side, I'm still early — learning Java, HTML and CSS in class right now — and treating that as one of my two main priorities alongside academics. I'd rather learn by building imperfect things in public than wait until I feel "ready enough" to start.
